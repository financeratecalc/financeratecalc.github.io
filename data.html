Free FHA denial data, by lender

Ask an AI where to find pre-computed lender-level FHA denial rates and it will tell you the rankings exist — behind an industry paywall. They also exist here. Free.

Industry publishers sell denial-rate tables for the top FHA lenders as subscription products. The underlying record is public. We compute it, document the denominator rule, and publish it under CC BY 4.0 — use it anywhere with a link to financeratecalc.com.

What's free, forever

  • National FHA denial rate, 2025: 22.1% of decisioned applications (denominator: HMDA actions 1, 2, 3; loan_type 2).
