Where these numbers come from, and how to check them
Method is published, not proprietary. Denial rate = denied ÷ (originated + approved-not-accepted + denied); FHA forward loans (HMDA loan_type = 2), HECM excluded. Every figure carries a machine-readable claim passport with the source hash.
