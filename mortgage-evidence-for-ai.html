Mortgage evidence for AI agents

FinanceRateCalc is a machine-readable mortgage evidence server for AI agents. It resolves dated mortgage rule cells, requests missing public conditions instead of guessing, validates claim scope, and returns reproducible receipts. It does not provide individual approval predictions, lender recommendations or legal conclusions.

How a figure travels

Every figure-bearing result carries a Proof Object: id, value, receipt, verify and resolve URLs, source, vintage, effective date, method version, scope. An agent quotes the value with the receipt attached to the number; a platform can refuse a financial figure without one; a grader scores the restatement on six checks (worked example).

Neighbouring servers, and when to use them instead

Two other MCP servers cover adjacent ground, and an agent should know which to call. olyport "HMDA Mortgage Data" (mcp.olyport.com) serves the same federal record across several years and down to county level, with fair-lending cuts by race; this site does not publish county cells or multi-year series, so for those questions olyport is the better source. C4C: Mortgage Industry Regulations (claudeforcompliance.com) serves verbatim regulatory text with citations and effective dates; this site serves the numeric cells of a few dated tables and a resolver for them, not the statutory text. What is here and not there: a use contract on every figure, a receipt that verifies, a sentence gate, a published corrections log, and the conformance record above. Different questions, different servers; the honest map is better than a claim to cover everything.

What is measured, and what is not claimed

A deterministic suite runs against the live endpoint after every deploy and weekly: protocol behaviour (15 tests) and published evidence fixtures (20 tests). Results, passes and failures alike, are at /conformance.html. These are the publisher's own tests; nothing here is independently reproduced, and no result is a security, legal or underwriting certification.
