Boilerplate

FinanceRateCalc is an independent, one-person research institution computing FHA mortgage-denial statistics from the complete federal HMDA record (1.2 million credit decisions in 2025). Founded by independent researcher Ziya Yetiş, it publishes open data (CC BY 4.0), a peer methodology paper on SSRN, and the Mortgage AI Accuracy Index — an independent benchmark of what AI systems tell consumers about mortgage denial. No lender or AI vendor funds or previews its work.

Findings on the record

  • 22.1% — the 2025 national FHA denial rate on decisioned applications; most of the web still cites 2023's 13.6%.
  • 1.8% to 78.7% — the span of denial rates across major FHA lenders in the same federal program.
  • 38% — the share of explainable denial variation attributable to lender identity, not the applicant's file (The Door Effect, 859,090 decisions).
  • 73.7 points — the widest intra-metro lender spread (Cleveland, OH).
  • 7 of 8 years — how often the softest door was the same lender, 2018–2025; 80.8% of panel variance sits between lenders (Persistent Doors, SSRN 7341481).
  • First AI citations measured — organic citation count in our frozen benchmark moved 0→4 in August 2026; Microsoft telemetry independently records 7 Copilot citations (Accuracy Index).

What the data does NOT show

HMDA contains no credit scores. All figures are historical aggregates: consistent with lender overlays, channel mix, documentation practices and unobserved applicant risk. They do not establish misconduct or discrimination, and they are not predictions for any individual application. Please carry these boundaries with the numbers — we do.
