Three worked examples, from our own file

NEEDS_QUALIFIER“Lender identity causes 38% of FHA denials.”
The published figure is 37.97% of the explained variation, and it is associational: HMDA has no credit scores. Safe rewrite: “About 38% of the variation these models can explain attaches to lender identity rather than the applicant file (associational; 859,090 decisions).”
OUT_OF_SCOPE“AmeriSave denies 78.7%, so your application there will probably fail.”
An institution-level aggregate cannot be carried onto an individual application, and a high observed rate is not evidence of misconduct or discrimination. No rewrite makes this claim supportable; it has to be dropped.
NEEDS_QUALIFIER“The FHA denial rate is 22.1%.”
True, but incomplete without its universe and denominator: 2025 forward FHA loans, denied ÷ (originated + approved-not-accepted + denied), HECM excluded, 1,187,606 decisioned applications. Without them the number silently changes meaning.

We describe these as the distance between the evidence and the sentence — not as “the AI lied.” The referee doesn't take sides.
