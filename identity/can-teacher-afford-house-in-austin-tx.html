📚 Teacher-Specific Mortgage Facts

Income note: Base salary only — summer income typically not counted unless 12-month contract.

Key challenge: Teachers in expensive cities (SF, NYC, LA) face the worst affordability gap of any profession relative to local home prices.

Your advantage: HUD Good Neighbor Next Door program: 50% off HUD homes in revitalization areas. Teacher-specific down payment assistance in many states.

The Real Math

On a $68,000 annual salary, your gross monthly income is $5,666. The safe housing threshold (28% front-end DTI) gives you a max monthly payment of $1,586. Austin's median home payment is $4,109/month — leaving you $2,069/month over budget.

Renting in Austin costs roughly $1,650/month for a 1BR. Buying at 36% DTI would require your maximum affordable price of $178,895 — below the city median.
