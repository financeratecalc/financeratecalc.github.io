VA loan denial rates in 2025: the national figure, the five outcomes, every state

Short answer. In 2025, 16.1% of decisioned VA applications in the United States were denied: 115,026 denials out of 712,121 applications that reached a credit decision (originated, approved but not accepted, or denied; reverse mortgages excluded). Receipt: ⟦FRC:va-national-denial-rate-2025:16.1%:32bdf09e⟧.

Read before comparing with FHA. The published FHA rate (22.1%) and this VA rate measure different applicant populations, defined by eligibility and composition. The difference is a fact about who applies for which program, not about which program or lender is easier. Every use contract on this page prohibits that inference. Universe: U-VA-2025-DECISIONED, declared before any figure was computed in provenance/universe-expansion-2025.md.
