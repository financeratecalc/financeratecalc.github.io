Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026
Short version: No account, no ads, no commission-earning links, no lead forms. Reading the site, checking a receipt and using the AI tools need no personal information. What is recorded is listed below, item by item, and the counts are public at /usage.

1. Who publishes this site

FinanceRateCalc (financeratecalc.com) is published by one person, Ziya Yetiş, from Türkiye. It restates figures from public US mortgage records and attaches a verifiable receipt to each. It is not a lender, a broker or an adviser, and it does not sell or pass on readers to anyone.

2. What is recorded, by what you do

Reading a page. Google Analytics 4 records the page, the referring site, the device type and an approximate location. There is no advertising network on this site.

Checking a receipt (the verify page, a receipt link, or the verify service called by a program). One line is stored per check: the day, the claim id, the result, the kind of requester (browser, script, or a named crawler), whether the request came from a page of this site, another site or directly, and the country. The IP address, the full user-agent string and the time of day are not stored.

Checking an AI answer (the “Check an answer” page). The text is examined in your browser. It is sent to us only if you press the button that records it as a misquote; then the quoted text, the name of the system you give and an optional link are stored and published in the misquote ledger. Who submitted it is not recorded.

Figure Radar notifications. If you turn them on, the push address your browser issues for this device and the reports you chose are stored, so that a notice can be sent when an official figure changes.

Witness (reading proofs). The service stores hashes of a document’s lines and the signed result of each reading. It does not store the document text, the statement text or any address.

Buying something. Lemon Squeezy is the seller of record and handles payment. We receive the order and the email address you give them; card details never reach us. For prepaid screening credits, the server stores a hash of the licence key and a counter, not the list that was screened and not the result.

Writing to us. The contact form is delivered by Formspree; we receive what you type. The newsletter is run on beehiiv, which holds the email address you subscribe with.

3. AI assistants and agents

This site can be used by AI systems in three ways, and each records the following.

  • The MCP server and the agent-to-agent endpoint (frc-mcp.ziyetis.workers.dev). When an assistant calls a tool, a daily counter is increased for the name the client software declares (for example the product name of the assistant) and the name of the tool. The question, the arguments of the call and the answer are not stored. Nothing identifies the person using the assistant, because the server never receives that information: it needs no login and asks for no personal data. The tools return published historical aggregates only; they do not accept details about an applicant.
  • Tools inside your browser (WebMCP). Where a browser lets an AI agent call tools on a page, this site offers three read-only ones (check a receipt, find a figure, get a figure). They run in your browser against the site’s public files and send nothing about you or your question to us or to anyone else.
  • Crawlers. AI and search crawlers may read every public page; robots.txt says so. When a crawler follows a receipt link, the check is recorded as described in section 2, with the crawler’s name.

The assistant you use (and the company that runs it) has its own privacy terms for what you type into it; those are outside this site’s control.

4. What this site does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising cookies
  • No commission-earning links and no lead generation: nothing you do here is passed to a lender or a vendor
  • No sale, rental or sharing of personal data
  • No collection of Social Security numbers, credit scores, income, or any detail of a loan application

5. Cookies and local storage

  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics 4. You may opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Local storage: preferences kept in your own browser and not sent to us.

6. Other companies involved

  • GitHub Pages hosts the pages; Cloudflare carries the traffic and runs the verify, MCP, Radar and Witness services. Both process requests, including IP addresses, as any host or network does.
  • Google Analytics 4 for usage statistics; Google Fonts for typefaces.
  • Lemon Squeezy for payments, Formspree for the contact form, beehiiv for the newsletter.

7. How long records are kept

  • Receipt checks and tool-call counters: about 400 days
  • Misquote ledger entries: about two years, and they are public
  • Witness records: hashes for about 180 days, signed results for about 400 days
  • Figure Radar: until you turn the notifications off or the browser withdraws the push address
  • Google Analytics: 14 months
  • Newsletter address: until you unsubscribe

8. Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you and ask for it to be deleted, wherever you live; California residents have these rights under the CCPA, and we do not sell personal data. Write to press@financeratecalc.com. Most of what this site records cannot be tied to a person, in which case we will say so.

9. Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. International readers

The site is published from Türkiye and mostly concerns US data. The companies listed in section 6 may process requests outside your country.

11. Changes and corrections

Changes are posted here with a new date. Corrected on November 23, 2026: the previous version of this page still described Google AdSense advertising and products that were withdrawn in September 2026. The site carries no ads; the page now describes what the site does today, including its services for AI agents.

12. Contact
