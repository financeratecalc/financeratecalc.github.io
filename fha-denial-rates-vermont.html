2025 federal record

FHA denial rate in Vermont: 26.9%

Of the 1,048 FHA applications that reached a credit decision in Vermont in 2025, 282 were denied. That is above the national rate of 21.7%, and ranks #48 of 52 jurisdictions from softest to hardest.

Loan size matters here too: applications under $150K were denied at 41.9% versus 21.6% for those above $250K — a 1.94× penalty on the cheapest way into a house.

What actually moves the odds

Geography sets the backdrop; the lender sets the outcome. Across the 100 largest FHA lenders nationally, 2025 denial rates ran from 1.8% to 78.7% — a 44× spread inside one federal program, far wider than any difference between states.
