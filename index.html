How to use it

The Denial Map™

Four steps, in order, if you have been denied — or want to avoid it.

01
Decode
Get the stated reason in writing — it is your right under federal law — then read it against that lender's own reason distribution. “Incomplete” means something very different at a door where it is 1.2% of denials than at one where it is 73.5%.
Reason fingerprints →
02
Year
Place the denial in its credit climate. A file refused in a tight year is not the same file in a loose one, and the record shows which year you were in.
The climate index →
03
Door
Compare lenders on observed outcomes, not advertising — nationally, in your metro, and by loan size. This is where the 44× lives.
Your metro's doors →
04
Reapply
Take the same file to a door whose record fits it. Consider applying to a second lender in parallel: if another door approves substantially the same file, you have learned the denial was about the door.
