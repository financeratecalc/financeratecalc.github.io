Freedom FHA Denial Rate (2025): the behavioral file

Direct answer · updated 2026-07-16

Freedom denied 24.2% of its FHA applications in 2025 — rank #5 of the 100 largest FHA lenders (1 = lowest denial), from CFPB HMDA loan-level records (1,187,606 decisioned FHA applications in 2025).

Sharpest observed DTI step: at CLTV 80-84, denial moves from 12.8% at DTI 45 to 23.2% at DTI 46 — a +10.4 point jump across one integer of DTI. FHA program rules permit DTI well above this; the desk does not.

Sharpest observed CLTV step: at DTI 43, denial moves from 5.4% in the 80-84% band to 96.1% in the 97+% band — +90.7 points on down payment alone.

Denial rates reflect applicant mix as well as underwriting strictness; overlays are lawful business decisions. Cells with n<50 excluded. Methodology
