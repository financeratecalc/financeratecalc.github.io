Official figures, restated as printed — with a receipt

What this is. Nothing on this page was computed by FinanceRateCalc. Each figure is copied from an official publication exactly as printed, with the document’s SHA-256, the table and row it sits in, its period, the publisher’s own exclusion note, and whether a later edition revised it. The receipt ⟦FRC:official-…⟧ attests only that this figure, with this definition and period, appears in that document with that hash. Use contracts say what each figure is not: an endorsement is not an HMDA origination or application; none of these is a denial rate. Verify any receipt at verify.html.

Why it exists: answer engines restate official figures with the wrong month, the wrong base or a superseded value. A receipt fixes the figure to its edition; a revision field says when the publisher changed it. Method: scripts/official_restate.py; data: data/official/; spec: Receipt 1.0.
