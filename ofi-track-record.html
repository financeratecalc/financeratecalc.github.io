How This Data Is Collected
After Zai provides lender comparison on observed data advice, users are asked: "Did this work? Track your outcome." Outcomes are self-reported by users and aggregated anonymously. No personally identifiable information is collected. All data is voluntary.

This is not a controlled study. Success rates reflect self-selected reporters and may not represent all users. The purpose is to build an empirical, transparent track record over time — not to claim scientific certainty.

The part almost nobody publishes free

Prices and rates are widely reported. Whether a lender says yes is not. In the complete 2025 federal record, denial rates across the 100 largest FHA lenders ran from 1.8% to 78.7% — same programme, same year.

And it is not simply who applies where: standardizing on state, loan amount, income, debt-to-income and loan-to-value, applicant mix explains only a 2.7× range in expected outcomes.
