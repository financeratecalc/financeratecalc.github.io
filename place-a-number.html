Which cells of this table produce that number?

An article says “12.8% were denied” and links to a table. Twelve point eight percent of what? Paste the table and the figure. The page tries the figure as a cell, as a share of a row or column total, as a ratio of two cells, as one cell over itself plus one or two others, as a percent change and as a difference, and lists every definition that reproduces it.

How to read the result

A match is not an attribution. It shows a definition that can produce the figure from this table, not the one its author used. Two definitions can match the same rounded figure; a large table will match some figures by coincidence, and the page says roughly how many to expect. No match is not proof of error: the figure may come from another table, another edition of the same report, or a definition this search does not try.

The figure is matched at the precision you type: 22.1% matches anything that rounds to 22.1, and 22.08% is stricter. Everything runs in this page; the table is not uploaded, stored or logged, so it is safe to use with a table you may not share. The code is one file.
