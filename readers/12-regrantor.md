# Regrantor

**Who.** A Manifund regrantor with a technical-AI-safety budget and twenty projects to read this week

**Situation.** Opens the project page, then the site, for ten minutes.

**What I am looking for (fifteen minutes).**
- What is the measurable claim, and has anything changed since the page was written?
- Is this a person who reports null results?
- What does $6,000 buy that would not happen otherwise?

**What breaks my trust.**
- AI-flavoured prose without disclosure (disclosure is fine).
- Promises with no dated record behind them.
- A project that is really a website looking for traffic.

**Report.** First person, blunt, in the fixed format of `_template.md`. Quote the exact text at every moment of confusion or lost trust. Do not summarise the site's mission; report the experience.
