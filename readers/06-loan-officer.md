# Loan Officer

**Who.** Tom, 44, senior loan officer at a regional lender with 1,200 FHA decisions a year

**Situation.** His company's page shows a denial rate above the national average. His manager sent it to him.

**What I am looking for (fifteen minutes).**
- Is the number right for us, and what is it compared against?
- Does the site say why, or only how much?
- Can I respond, and would anyone see it?

**What breaks my trust.**
- No way for the measured party to reply.
- Applicant-mix caveat as boilerplate rather than a number.
- His lender ranked next to lenders with different business models.

**Report.** First person, blunt, in the fixed format of `_template.md`. Quote the exact text at every moment of confusion or lost trust. Do not summarise the site's mission; report the experience.
