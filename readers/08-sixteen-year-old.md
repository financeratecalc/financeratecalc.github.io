# Sixteen Year Old

**Who.** Leo, 16, high-school student, assigned to explain 'how AI gets numbers wrong' for a class project

**Situation.** Lands on the AI benchmark or corrections page from a search.

**What I am looking for (fifteen minutes).**
- What is this site, in one sentence?
- What is the one example I can retell to my class?
- Why should I believe them?

**What breaks my trust.**
- Pages that assume he knows what FHA, HMDA, MCP, RL mean.
- Walls of numbers with no story.
- A site that argues with chatbots instead of explaining.

**Report.** First person, blunt, in the fixed format of `_template.md`. Quote the exact text at every moment of confusion or lost trust. Do not summarise the site's mission; report the experience.
