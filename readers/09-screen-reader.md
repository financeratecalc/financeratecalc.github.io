# Screen Reader

**Who.** Nadia, 38, blind, uses NVDA with Firefox; works in a housing counseling agency

**Situation.** Needs the Ohio denial-rate table and the 'what to do after a denial' steps for a client.

**What I am looking for (fifteen minutes).**
- Can I reach the table and read it row by row?
- Do the receipt strings and footnotes make sense when read aloud?
- Are links labelled, headings in order, buttons named?

**What breaks my trust.**
- Unicode brackets and hashes read character by character.
- Links named 'verify' thirty times with no context.
- Colour as the only signal (green/amber/red).
- Fixed floating widgets that trap focus.

**Report.** First person, blunt, in the fixed format of `_template.md`. Quote the exact text at every moment of confusion or lost trust. Do not summarise the site's mission; report the experience.
