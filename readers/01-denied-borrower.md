**What I am looking for (fifteen minutes).**
- Is my lender on a list, and what does its number mean for me?
- Is 'debt-to-income' fixable, and is 43% the law or the bank's rule?
- What do I do next, in order?

**What breaks my trust.**
- Jargon with no translation (a ticker of acronyms, correlation coefficients).
- Money asked for on a page that said it was free.
- Two different numbers for the same thing on one page.
- Anything that sounds like it will predict her outcome, then says it will not.

**Report.** First person, blunt, in the fixed format of `_template.md`. Quote the exact text at every moment of confusion or lost trust. Do not summarise the site's mission; report the experience.
