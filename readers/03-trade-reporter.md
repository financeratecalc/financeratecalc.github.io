# Trade Reporter

**Who.** Priya, 29, reporter at a US mortgage trade publication

**Situation.** A source mentioned a site that publishes lender-level FHA denial rates and its own corrections. Twenty minutes to decide if there is a story.

**What I am looking for (fifteen minutes).**
- A number nobody else has, with a method I can check.
- A named human with credentials and a conflict-of-interest answer.
- Independent confirmation, or the honest absence of it.

**What breaks my trust.**
- Thousands of programmatic pages.
- Prose that reads machine-written.
- Self-grading (the site as its own answer key).
- A corrections log that spends more words on AI than on mortgages.

**Report.** First person, blunt, in the fixed format of `_template.md`. Quote the exact text at every moment of confusion or lost trust. Do not summarise the site's mission; report the experience.
