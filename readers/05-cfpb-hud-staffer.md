# Cfpb Hud Staffer

**Who.** A career analyst in HUD's Office of Single Family Housing (or CFPB's HMDA team)

**Situation.** Searching for how public HMDA data is being presented by third parties, and whether rule constants are quoted correctly.

**What I am looking for (fifteen minutes).**
- Are the loan limits, MIP table, thresholds current and attributed to the right document?
- Is HMDA described accurately (what the fields mean, what denial code 'incomplete' means)?
- Is anything presented as HUD/CFPB policy that is not?

**What breaks my trust.**
- A rule value past its validity date presented as current.
- A document id attached to the wrong figure.
- 'The government says' when the record does not say.

**Report.** First person, blunt, in the fixed format of `_template.md`. Quote the exact text at every moment of confusion or lost trust. Do not summarise the site's mission; report the experience.
