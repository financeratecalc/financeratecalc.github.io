USDA loan denial rates in 2025: the national figure, the five outcomes, every state

Short answer. In 2025, 12.9% of decisioned USDA applications in the United States were denied: 6,031 denials out of 46,869 applications that reached a credit decision (originated, approved but not accepted, or denied; reverse mortgages excluded). Receipt: ⟦FRC:usda-national-denial-rate-2025:12.9%:1d75c127⟧.

Read before comparing with FHA. The published FHA rate (22.1%) and this USDA rate measure different applicant populations, defined by eligibility and composition. The difference is a fact about who applies for which program, not about which program or lender is easier. Every use contract on this page prohibits that inference. Purchase-only and refinance-only rates for USDA are not published yet; the denominator map covers FHA and VA. Universe: U-USDA-2025-DECISIONED, declared before any figure was computed in provenance/universe-expansion-2025.md.
