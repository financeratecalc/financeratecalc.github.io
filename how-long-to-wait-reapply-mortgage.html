Depends entirely on the reason

How long should you wait to reapply? It depends on the sentence in your letter.

Short answer

There is no mandatory waiting period after a mortgage denial. Nothing in federal rules requires one. What determines the right interval is the reason you were given — and for some reasons the answer is not "wait" at all, but "different lender."

By reason

Reason on your letterTypical intervalWhat actually has to change
Incomplete applicationDaysUsually documents, not qualifications — and at most lenders this reason is rare (1.8% median), which makes it worth asking why it was used
Unverifiable informationDays to weeksSourcing a deposit, documenting income, a written explanation
Collateral / appraisalNoneThe property, not you. A different property, or a renegotiation
Insufficient cashWeeks to monthsFunds for closing, or an assistance program
Debt-to-income1–3 monthsPaying down a balance, or documenting income that was not counted
Credit historyVaries widelyDepends entirely on what is on the report
Employment historyMonthsTime in role, or documentation of continuity

The interval nobody mentions: zero

For several of the reasons above, the honest answer is that waiting changes nothing, because the obstacle was never on your side of the table. Denial rates across the 100 largest FHA lenders in 2025 ranged from 1.8% to 78.7%, and in the typical US metro the softest and hardest high-volume lender were 22.2 points apart. Applying elsewhere costs you nothing on your credit report — the next lender cannot see the refusal, and inquiries inside the shopping window count as one. Why a denial does not hurt your score.

Before you reapply anywhere

  1. Get the reasons in writing. You are entitled to them within 30 days, specifically, not as a generic form.
  2. Check how often that reason is used by that lender compared with its peers. Some reasons are near-universal; one is concentrated in a handful of institutions.
  3. Look at your own metro rather than the national table — the spread inside a single city is often wider than the national one.
