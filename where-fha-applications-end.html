Where FHA applications end: the five outcomes the record keeps

Short answer. A denial rate counts one of five ends. In 2025, lenders acted on 1,563,370 FHA applications (reverse mortgages excluded; purchased loans and preapproval requests not counted). Of those, 56.4% were originated, 17.7% were withdrawn by the applicant, 16.8% were denied, 6.4% were closed for incompleteness and 2.8% were approved but not accepted. More FHA applications were withdrawn than denied. The site’s published denial rate, 22.1%, is the same denials over a smaller base: the 1,187,606 applications that reached a credit decision (originated, approved-not-accepted, denied). Both are right; they answer different questions (why FHA denial rates differ, question 5).
