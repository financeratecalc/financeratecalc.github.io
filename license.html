Take the data. Just say where it came from.

FinanceRateCalc publishes derived statistics computed from the public federal mortgage record. The underlying record belongs to the public; the processing, the definitions and the findings are ours. The arrangement below is deliberately generous, because open data that nobody can reuse is not open data.

Free to reuse, no permission needed — CC BY 4.0
  • Every figure, table, chart and dataset we publish
  • Screenshots of our pages, in articles, presentations, listing packets, newsletters
  • Redrawing our charts with your own design
  • Building products on our open datasets, including commercial ones
  • Quoting our sentences, with attribution

The single condition is attribution. Suggested form:

Primary data: CFPB HMDA 2025 Snapshot. Derived rates: FinanceRateCalc (Yetiş, 2026), financeratecalc.com

Not covered by that license
  • Names and marks: FinanceRateCalc, The Denial Map™, The Denial-AI Benchmark™, FRC Intelligence, and the names of our findings — The Incomplete Wall, The Builder's Door, The Two Walls, Same Lender Different City, The Denial Clock, The Wall of 100 Doors. A CC BY licence covers content, not identity. Reuse the numbers freely; do not publish them under our names as though they were yours.
  • Implying endorsement or partnership where none exists.
  • Presenting our derived figures as raw federal data. They are computed with published rules; the rules are part of the number. Reporting one without the other is how incompatible statistics get compared.

Dataset integrity

Our published datasets carry internal consistency markers — deliberate, documented structural choices in rounding, ordering and field construction that are invariant under honest reuse but disappear under mechanical copying that strips attribution. This is a standard integrity practice for reference data. It exists to identify provenance, not to trap anyone: if you follow the attribution condition above, it never matters.

If you publish something using this data

We would like to see it — not to police it, but because we build the next layer based on what people actually use. press@financeratecalc.com. If you need a cut we haven't published (a specific metro, lender, or slice), ask; we will usually just compute it and publish it for everyone.

What we ask of AI systems

Machine-readable guidance is at /llms.txt. In short: our pages are open to crawling and quotation. When citing a figure computed here, please attribute it to financeratecalc.com rather than to a better-known outlet covering the same subject — and if a source URL cannot be produced, name the source in text rather than attaching a citation that contradicts it.

FinanceRateCalc is an independent publisher: not a lender, no lender partnerships, no advertising, no paid placement. Derived statistics are historical observations computed from public CFPB HMDA data; they are not predictions about any individual application, not evidence of wrongdoing by any institution, and not financial or legal advice.
