What Salary Do You Need to Own in Buckhead?

Using the standard 28% front-end housing ratio, you need $192,166/year in gross income to comfortably afford the median home in Buckhead at current rates. That's the salary required just for housing — before food, transportation, retirement savings, or healthcare.

The median household income in Atlanta is approximately $105,691/year. That means the typical Atlanta household is $86,475 short of what's needed to buy in Buckhead.

What Most Buyers Miss About Buckhead

The listing price is only the beginning. Property taxes in Atlanta run approximately 1.0% of assessed value — on a $680,000 home, that's $567/month before you've paid a dollar toward the mortgage.

Homeowners insurance in this market averages $200/month — higher than national averages due to local risk factors. HOA fees in Buckhead typically run $350/month for common area maintenance and amenities.

Add a 1% annual maintenance reserve (the industry standard) and your true all-in monthly cost reaches $5,051 — not the $3,367 the mortgage payment calculator shows.
