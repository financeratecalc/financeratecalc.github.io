conventional loan denial rates in 2025: the national figure, the five outcomes, every state

Short answer. In 2025, 23.4% of decisioned conventional applications in the United States were denied: 1,726,956 denials out of 7,376,124 applications that reached a credit decision (originated, approved but not accepted, or denied; reverse mortgages and open-end lines of credit excluded). Receipt: ⟦FRC:conv-national-denial-rate-2025:23.4%:14988d1a⟧.

Read before comparing with FHA. The published FHA rate (22.1%) and this conventional rate measure different applicant populations, defined by eligibility and composition. The difference is a fact about who applies for which program, not about which program or lender is easier. Every use contract on this page prohibits that inference. Purchase-only and refinance-only rates for conventional are not published yet; the denominator map covers FHA and VA. What this rate covers: every closed-end conventional application that reached a decision, for any loan purpose (home purchase, refinance, cash-out refinance, home improvement, other) and any lien position or property type. Conventional denial rates quoted elsewhere usually describe first-lien home-purchase mortgages only, a narrower set; they are not comparable with this figure. Universe: U-CONV-2025-DECISIONED, declared before any figure was computed in provenance/universe-expansion-2025.md.
