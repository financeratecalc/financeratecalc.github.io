Figure Radar · free · no e-mail, no account

Be told when an official report changes a number

Agencies print a figure one month and sometimes print a different value for the same month in the next edition, without a note. If you quoted the first value, nothing tells you. This does: choose the reports you rely on, and this device gets a notification when one of them prints a new edition or changes a figure it had already printed.

Checking this device…
On an iPhone or iPad: tap the share button, choose “Add to Home Screen”, open the page from the new icon, then turn notifications on. Apple allows web notifications only for pages added that way.

What is stored

For each device: the push address your browser hands out, the reports you ticked, and the date. No e-mail address, no name, no IP address, no cookie. The notification itself carries no content; when it arrives, the page asks the server what happened and shows it. Turning it off deletes the entry. How many devices are subscribed is public: counts only. The same events are readable by machines at /radar/events and in the revision ledger.

A notification says that a report was restated here and what changed between two editions. It does not say why the agency changed a figure, and it is not advice about any loan. The check runs once an hour; an agency can publish and this page can lag by that long, or by longer if the agency moves a file.
