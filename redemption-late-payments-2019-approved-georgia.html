What Actually Changed

Lates were from 2019 — over 4 years old. National bank still flagged them. FHA lender only cares about last 12 months of payment history. Zero recent lates = approved.

🏦 Banker's Take

FHA underwriting focuses heavily on the last 12-24 months. Older derogatory marks carry much less weight. If your recent payment history is clean, FHA may approve where conventional lenders won't.

The Pattern This Story Reveals

This story follows the most common pattern in our database: the problem wasn't the borrower's finances. It was which lender they walked into first. Different lender types have fundamentally different underwriting criteria — and most applicants don't know this until after the denial.
