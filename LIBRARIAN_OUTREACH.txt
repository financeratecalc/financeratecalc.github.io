Subject: Free open dataset for your [real estate / housing] guide — federal mortgage denial data (CC BY 4.0)

Dear [Name],

I came across your [Real Estate / Housing / Business] research guide while looking for where
researchers find processed HMDA data, and I thought this might be useful for it.

I publish a free, openly licensed dataset derived from the complete CFPB Home Mortgage
Disclosure Act record: FHA mortgage denial statistics at lender, metro and state level,
including denial-reason distributions and a peer-standardized measure. The 2025 file covers
1,217,297 decisioned applications. It is archived with a DOI and mirrored on Hugging Face:

  Catalog:     https://financeratecalc.com/data-catalog.html
  DOI:         https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21575106
  Methodology: https://financeratecalc.com/methodology.html

Why it may be worth a line in a guide: the raw HMDA file is roughly two gigabytes and
effectively unusable without processing, so students routinely stall at that step. This is
that processing, with the computation rules published so results are reproducible from the
public source. Everything is CC BY 4.0 — no registration, no paywall, no account.

I am an independent researcher with a career on the credit side of banking. The site carries
no advertising, sells nothing on the data pages, and takes no lender funding; I mention this
only because "free mortgage data" usually means something else.

If it is not a fit for your guide, no reply needed at all. If it is, and you would like a
different cut of the data for a course or a student project, I am glad to compute it.

With thanks for what subject guides do,

Ziya Yetis
FinanceRateCalc — independent research
press@financeratecalc.com
