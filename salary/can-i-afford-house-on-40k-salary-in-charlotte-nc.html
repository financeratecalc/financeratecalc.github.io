What This Means For You

On a $40K salary, Charlotte's median home is currently out of reach. Your DTI would hit 72.8%, well above what any lender will approve. In this market, your salary supports homes up to $152,502. Consider neighboring cities, a co-borrower, or a 2-year income growth plan.

The Credit Score Factor

At $40K income, your credit score determines your rate — and your rate determines how much home you can buy. The difference between a 620 and 740 score on a $312,000 loan is approximately $291/month and $104,803 over 30 years.
