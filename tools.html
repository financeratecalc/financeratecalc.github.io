Tools

The tools this site maintains. Each one reads the public federal record or an official report, says where the evidence ends, and carries receipts for its figures. None of them predicts how one application will be decided or tells you which lender to choose.

Check a number

For a figure you read in an article or an AI answer.

  • Check a receiptIs the number beside a receipt still the one the publisher issued, or was it changed or corrected?
  • Check an AI answerPaste an answer about FHA denials or mortgage rules: what each number carries, and what the sentence added that the number cannot.
  • One figure, every checkOne official figure followed from the source report to an AI decision, with your browser checking the signature itself.

After a denial

What the record can tell you, and what only the lender can.

  • The Denial MapFour steps after a denial: what the stated reason means, what the record says about the year and the lender, and what to ask in writing.
  • Denial code lookupThe adverse action reason codes, in plain English.
  • Evidence NavigatorA question mapped onto the federal record: what the evidence supports, with receipts, and when the question needs a licensed professional instead.

What the federal record says

2025 HMDA, FHA loans; historical aggregates with their denominators.

  • FHA denial rates, 100 largest lendersEach rate with its applications, its year and its receipt. A rate describes a lender’s applicant pool, not how it would decide one file.
  • FHA denial rates by stateAll states with loan-size splits.
  • Metro gapsHow far apart lenders’ denial rates are inside one metro.

Official figures and their revisions

Monthly reports of HUD, Ginnie Mae and the SBA, restated as printed.

  • Official figuresEach figure as printed, with the document’s hash, table and row.
  • Revision ledgerFigures a later edition printed differently, without a revision mark.
  • Figure RadarBe told when an official report changes a number.

For newsrooms, researchers and AI agents

  • For newsroomsA figure in an article that says when its source has changed.
  • Cite a findingThe sentence, the receipt and the attribution, ready to paste.
  • Open dataThe datasets behind every page, free under CC BY 4.0.
  • For AI agentsA tool server that returns each figure with its receipt.

  • Mortgage calculatorPayment, amortization and total interest. Arithmetic on your inputs, not a judgement of whether a lender would approve them.
