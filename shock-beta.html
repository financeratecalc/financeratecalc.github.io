This page has been retired.

This page gave eleven lenders a “Shock Beta” score from 0 to 100, described as a world-first measure of how violently each lender’s underwriting reacts to economic shocks, and said the score had been validated at r = +0.946. On 28 November 2026 the scores were recomputed from the page’s own data file with the method the page stated, and they did not come out as published: Freedom 100 and NewRez 90 on the page, NewRez 100 and Freedom 99 recomputed; Rocket 9 on the page, 16 recomputed; loanDepot 24 on the page, 37 recomputed. The page did not show enough to check its correlation, and a correlation across eleven lenders could not carry the prediction it was used for. A score that does not reproduce from its own data cannot be defended, and this site does not forecast how named lenders will behave. The page was withdrawn on 28 November 2026 and now records that.

What this site does instead: it shows what the record supports and where the evidence ends — The Denial Map · Evidence Navigator · FHA denial rates by lender, with receipts · Corrections log.

The data behind everything remains free.
