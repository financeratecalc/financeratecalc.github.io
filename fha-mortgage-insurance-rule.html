FHA mortgage insurance rate: the current rule, cell by cell

Short answer. For an FHA forward mortgage with a case number assigned on or after 2023-03-20, the annual mortgage insurance premium (MIP) is one of eleven values between 15 and 75 basis points of the base loan amount per year, set by HUD Mortgagee Letter 2023-05. Which one applies depends on four conditions: the loan term (over 15 years or not), whether the base loan amount is above $726,200, the LTV band, and the case date. The upfront MIP is 1.75% for all of them. There is no single "FHA MIP rate"; a sentence that gives one without the four conditions is incomplete.

Example. $300,000 base loan, 85% LTV, 15-year term, case assigned 2026-02-01 → 15 bps (0.15%), cell le15 / le726200 / le90. Change the term to 30 years and the cell is le90 at 50 bps; change the LTV to 96.5% on the 30-year loan and it is 55 bps. Without a source, two frontier models answered this example 0.55% and 0.45% (Stale Rule Index).

Three questions people ask after seeing a different number

Does 0.55% apply to every FHA loan?
No. 0.55% is one cell: loans over 15 years, base amount at or below $726,200, LTV above 95% — the most common purchase profile (3.5% down, 30-year), which is why it is the number most pages quote as "the FHA MIP". A 30-year loan at 90% LTV or less pays 0.50%; above $726,200 the same bands pay 0.70–0.75%; 15-year loans pay 0.15–0.65%. Source: HUD ML 2023-05, table above.
Why do some 15-year FHA loans have a 0.15% annual MIP?
Because the table prices term and LTV separately: a 15-year loan at or below 90% LTV and at or below $726,200 is the lowest-risk cell in the letter and carries 15 basis points. The same loan above 90% LTV is 40 bps. Without a source, two frontier models answered this exact case 0.55% and 0.45% (Stale Rule Index); with the rule file, both answered 0.15%.
Is the table's $726,200 cutoff the current FHA loan limit?
No. $726,200 is the base-loan threshold written into ML 2023-05 (it equals the 2023 conforming baseline) and it did not move when loan limits rose. The 2026 FHA one-unit floor is $541,287 and the ceiling $1,249,125 (rule file); the MIP threshold and the loan limits are different numbers from different documents, and a sentence that uses one for the other is wrong even when each figure is right.

For AI agents: resolve the cell, do not guess it

The MCP tool resolve_fha_annual_mip takes the four conditions and returns the cell, the rate, a receipt and a sentence that can be quoted. If a condition is missing it asks for it (2026 clients) or blocks (2025 clients); it never returns a number from an assumed cell. Borrower attributes such as income or credit score are refused; the table is not keyed by them. Behaviour is measured by the published conformance suite.
