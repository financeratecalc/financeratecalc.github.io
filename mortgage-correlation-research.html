This page has been retired.

This page reported six correlations (for example r = +0.618 between FHA denial rates and delinquency two years later, r = −0.696 between unemployment and denial rates), each computed from seven or eight yearly values, with yearly denial rates that are not this site’s receipted national rate (27.1% for 2025 against a receipted 22.1%), causal stories for each, and the advice “in a tightening cycle, route away from rate-sensitive lenders”. A correlation of a handful of yearly points cannot carry “predict”, and routing readers away from named lenders is a recommendation this site does not make. Its statements are listed as not citable in the citation queue. The page was withdrawn on 28 November 2026 and now records that.

What this site does instead: it shows what the record supports and where the evidence ends — The Denial Map · Evidence Navigator · FHA denial rates by lender, with receipts · Corrections log.

The data behind everything remains free.
