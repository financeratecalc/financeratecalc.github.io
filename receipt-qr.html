Put a number on paper that your reader can check

A statistic in a flyer, a market report or a slide cannot update itself. Print this code next to it. Anyone who scans the code sees, at that moment, whether the figure still stands in the federal record, or has been corrected since you printed it.

How to use it

Use the sentence as written, with the figure beside the code. Print the code at 2 cm (about 0.8 in) or larger, with white space around it. If the figure is ever corrected, every copy already printed will say so when scanned, and show the new value. If someone changes the number next to the code, the scan says that too.

What these figures are. Shares of applications that reached a credit decision in 2025, from the public federal HMDA file. They describe what happened to all applicants together. They do not predict what will happen to one application, and they do not compare lenders. Rates for different programs describe different groups of applicants and are not a ranking of programs. Lender-level figures are not offered here on purpose.

The code holds a link to financeratecalc.com/q.html with the figure’s receipt. Nothing about the person scanning is collected. Check any receipt by hand: verify. All figures: denominator map.
