The Denial Genome: what the record shows, and what this page used to say

Withdrawn. From June 2026 this page said that each FHA lender “rejects on” one characteristic reason, that the pattern had been stable for eight years, and that a borrower should match a file’s weakness to a lender. None of that stands. The named reasons do not match this site’s own 2025 reason shares for 3 of the four lenders it named: Rocket Mortgage (page said credit history; most cited in 2025: collateral, 44.9%); Freedom Mortgage (page said incomplete application; most cited in 2025: collateral, 44.3%); Wells Fargo Bank (page said credit history; most cited in 2025: debt-to-income ratio, 49.3%). The stability claim was never published with data behind it. Matching a file to a lender is advice about one application, which aggregate records cannot support and this site does not give. The page’s LSI scores were not receipted figures and are removed. Correction entry.

What these figures do not establish

Reason codes are filed by lenders under federal reporting rules. They describe what was reported, not why an underwriter decided, and they partly reflect who applies to each lender, through which channel and for which product. A lender’s most-cited reason says nothing about how one application would be treated there. Nothing here ranks lenders or recommends one.
