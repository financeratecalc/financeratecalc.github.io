Ark-La-Tex Financial Services LLC: FHA denial rate in 2025

Direct answer · updated 2026-07-19

Ark-La-Tex Financial Services LLC’s FHA denial rate in 2025 was 4.6%.

Ark-La-Tex Financial Services LLC denied 4.6% of its 4,671 decisioned FHA applications in 2025 — rank #90 of the 100 largest FHA lenders, below the national rate of 22.1%.
