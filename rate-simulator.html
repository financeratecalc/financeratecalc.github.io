This page has been retired.

This page offered a slider and a “prediction model” that said which lenders would “survive” a change in rates. That is a forecast about named lenders, drawn from a model whose figures carried no receipt and no stated error; this site neither ranks lenders nor predicts what they will do. The page was withdrawn on 27 November 2026 and now records that.

What this site does instead: it prints a number only if it carries a receipt or is named as an assumption, and it shows where the evidence ends — The Denial Map · FHA denial rates by state · Check an answer · Corrections log.

The retirement is recorded in the corrections log. The data behind everything remains free.
