Why lenders say no: the denial reason fingerprint

Direct answer · rebuilt from the lender receipts

Two lenders can post the same denial rate for different reasons — and the federal record shows each lender’s stated-reason fingerprint. In 2025, among the 20 largest FHA lenders, the ones with the lowest observed denial rates (CrossCountry, Guild, Fairway, under 10%) cited mostly underwriting math: debt-to-income on about 40.7% of their denials, credit history on about 25.9%. The lenders with the highest rates cited incomplete applications far more often: 75.2% of Carrington Mortgage Services LLC’s denials, 48.3% of AmeriSave Mortgage Company’s, 30.7% of Better Mortgage Corporation’s, against a median of 2.8% across the 100 largest. Part of the denial-rate spread records what happened to the file, not a judgement of the applicant. Source: 1,187,606 decisioned FHA applications, CFPB HMDA 2025.

Three patterns in the record

① Where rates are low, denials look like underwriting. Among the low-rate lenders in the table, debt-to-income and credit history lead the stated reasons. Where rates are highest, the leading reason is often that the file was closed incomplete. A denial code records the outcome, not the cause: an incomplete file may reflect applicant abandonment, documentation timelines or channel design.

② Rocket Mortgage’s leading reason is the property. Collateral appears on 44.9% of its denials, ahead of incomplete (25.0%) and debt-to-income (18.0%).

③ Builder-affiliated lenders cite the math. Debt-to-income appears on 62.3% of Lennar Mortgage, LLC’s denials and 59.1% of DHI Mortgage Company, LTD.’s, while collateral is near zero (0.4% and 0.1%) — consistent with new construction clearing appraisal.

What this is not

Reason codes are filed by lenders under federal reporting rules; shares describe reporting patterns, not intent, negligence or service quality, and they partly reflect applicant, product and channel mix. A high incomplete share is an observation about funnel outcomes, not an accusation. Nothing here ranks lenders by quality or recommends one.
