The Echo Ledger

Statistics travel. As they travel, they mutate: qualifiers fall off, association becomes causation, an aggregate becomes someone's odds, a source gets invented. This ledger tracks the journey of our flagship FHA statistics through the web and the AI answer-space — each echo dated, evidenced, and classified against its published Claim Contract. To our knowledge, no one else maps statistic mutation this way — because doing so requires owning the ground truth and the contracts.

What this is not. Not an "AI error hunt," not a shaming board. Verdicts apply to sentences, not vendors; every classification is deterministic and reproducible from the public contracts. A denial is a data point — so is an echo.
